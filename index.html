Episodes

Four stories I'd pick if you only had ten minutes. Problem, what I did, what changed.

E013 min

One User, One Agent

Sole developer · AI agents · Recommendations · WhatsApp & voice

Problem
One home page for everyone is a poor fit when people come to STAGE for shows in very different dialects, with very different tastes.
What I did
Built 1U1A on my own: a dedicated AI agent per user that picks their recommendations, talks to them on WhatsApp and voice, and reaches out first when there's something worth watching.
Result
Runs at ~1 lakh generations a day, and still builds a personalised home page in ~5 seconds at that scale.
E022 min

The 19% who never logged in

Funnel analysis · Auth · Meta, Google & Snapchat ads

Problem
Digging through the Meta-ads funnel myself, I found that 19% of people who installed from an ad never made it past login.
What I did
Auto-login. The ad deeplink carries a random, opaque hash instead of a user ID or phone number. The hash maps to the user in our DB, logs them in on first open, and the entry is deleted right after.
Result
Login conversion went from 81% to 87% for users from Meta, Google and Snapchat ads.
E033 min

Promo in, ads out

S3 presigned URLs · Meta, Google & Snapchat APIs · Job queues

Problem
Promo makers upload a video and we create draft ads on Meta, Google and Snapchat. It ran one by one: server → S3 → Meta, about 2 minutes each. At ~200 promos a day, the average wait was ~20 minutes.
What I did
Uploads go straight from the browser to S3 with presigned URLs. Meta gets a 10-minute presigned link instead of the file passing through our server, and everything runs in parallel. Added AI-written ad copy, plus PostgreSQL-backed jobs with idempotent submissions, resumable checkpoints and selective retries.
Result
Promos run in parallel instead of waiting in line, and the pipeline now handles ~250 promos a day.
E042 min

The TV app, take two

Samsung Tizen · LG webOS · React · OTA updates

Problem
Our Samsung and LG TV apps first shipped as the website itself, running on the TV. It worked. But getting a release out to TVs took about two months.
What I did
Nobody asked, but I rewrote it for TV in React. Then I added OTA delivery, so the app pulls new web bundles itself.
Result
Loads ~4s faster, responds to clicks ~60% faster, and releases without native changes now take ~2 minutes instead of ~2 months.
