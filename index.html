Problem
We were paying a third-party tool every month for campaigns we could run ourselves.
What I did
Built the platform on NestJS, PostgreSQL, Kafka, ClickHouse and Temporal, with idempotent requests so nothing sends twice, per-recipient tracking, and retry workflows for the sends that fail.

STAGEBackendAWS S3Ads APIs

Promo-to-ad pipeline

Promo makers upload a video; draft ads appear on Meta, Google and Snapchat.

Problem
Every upload went server → S3 → Meta, about 2 minutes each, one after another. At ~200 promos a day the average wait was ~20 minutes.
What I did
Moved uploads to go straight from the browser to S3 with presigned URLs, handed Meta a 10-minute presigned link instead of the file, and processed everything in parallel. Added AI-written ad copy, and PostgreSQL-backed jobs with idempotent submissions, resumable checkpoints and selective retries.
Result
Uploads run in parallel instead of queueing, and the pipeline now handles ~250 promos a day.

STAGEAuthFunnel analysis

Auto-login for users from ads

I found the drop-off myself, digging through the Meta-ads funnel: 19% of people who came in from an ad never got past login.

What I did
Built the backend and integrations for auto-login. Each ad deeplink carries a random hash instead of a user ID or phone number; the hash maps to the user in our DB, and the entry is deleted once they're in.
Result
Login conversion went from 81% to 87% for users from Meta, Google and Snapchat ads.
