02 / FIELD NOTES

The decision
is the work.

Short notes on the tradeoffs behind the systems above. Open one to see what changed.

NOTE 01 / WEBPerformance is a product feature.+

Problem The first load was slow, and the web journey had to carry users through to install or subscription.

Decision Work on rendering strategy, caching, accessibility, tests, and the funnel together—not as separate tickets.

Result Lighthouse performance went from about 58 to 90, LCP from 7s to 3s, and web-to-app conversion from 27% to 42%.

NOTE 02 / CAMPAIGNSOwn the data path.+

Problem Third-party campaign tooling was expensive and limited what we could control.

Decision Build an in-house alternative: NestJS APIs, Kafka events, ClickHouse audience data, and Temporal execution.

Result The platform reduced monthly tooling cost by ₹4 lakh.

NOTE 03 / VIDEOA migration needs an escape hatch.+

Problem Video transcoding costs added up as the catalogue grew.

Decision Replace AWS MediaConvert with self-hosted FFmpeg, while building gradual rollout, re-transcodes without downtime, and health visibility.

Result Approximately 70% lower transcoding cost.

NOTE 04 / TVRelease speed changes the product.+

Problem TV releases could take around two months.

Decision Build OTA updates into the Samsung and LG TV apps.

Result Release time fell to about two minutes.
